# Sitting Pretty — Ke'Ebonie Hill

register: brand

## Product Purpose
Standalone booking site replacing Ke'Ebonie Hill's StyleSeat profile. StyleSeat buries her in marketplace search and skims her money; this site is HER destination: her name, her prices, her policies, direct text-to-book. Client goal: people find her, see the work, book without a middleman.

## Users
Black women (and moms booking for kids) in Fort Worth / DFW, ages ~18-45, browsing on phones at night after seeing a style on Instagram. They scroll, they don't hunt through menus. They care about: what styles she does, exact prices and deposits, how to book fast, proof she's good (4.9 stars, 138 reviews, 1K+ appointments, 10 years experience).

## Brand & Tone
Warm, confident, personal. Ebony's own voice: "sit in my chair, we talk and share life while I bless your hair." Glam but never cold or corporate. She books one client at a time, kids welcome. It should feel like HER chair, not a platform.

## Anti-references
- StyleSeat itself: generic marketplace chrome, her name in tiny text under platform branding.
- Beauty-template pink/rose-gold sites. Reflex aesthetic, avoid.
- Cheap linktree-style link piles.

## Strategic principles
- Every price, duration, deposit, policy comes verbatim from her StyleSeat data. Nothing invented.
- Booking = text or call (817) 704-8300 with the service pre-filled. No fake booking engine.
- Scroll-first, mobile-first. Intro animation + micro-interactions carry the wow.
- Her real client photos only (from her own gallery). Never text over faces.
