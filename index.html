Your deposit books your spot

15-minute grace period

After 15 minutes the appointment is cancelled, and a $25 rescheduling fee applies before you can book again.

Bring your hair on time

Wigs, closures, frontals, and bundles need to arrive in the time frame you discuss with Ebony so she can customize them. Otherwise the appointment is rescheduled, or a $25 same-day customization fee applies.
