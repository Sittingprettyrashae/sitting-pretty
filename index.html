Before you bookHouse rules, so we both sit pretty.

1

Deposits are due within 24 hours

A deposit secures every appointment and comes off your balance the day of your service. Unpaid after 24 hours, the appointment is cancelled.

2

15-minute grace period

After 15 minutes the appointment is cancelled, and a $25 rescheduling fee applies before you can book again.

3

Bring your hair on time

Wigs, closures, frontals, and bundles need to arrive in the time frame you discuss with Ebony so she can customize them. Otherwise the appointment is rescheduled, or a $25 same-day customization fee applies.

4

Feeling sick? Reschedule

If you have a cough or aren’t feeling well, please move your appointment. Everybody’s health matters in a one-on-one studio.

5

Kids are welcome

Ebony is a mother and knows a sitter isn’t always easy to find. Bring them along.
